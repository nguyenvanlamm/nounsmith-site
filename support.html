Nounsmith Support

A riddle book in four chapters, for iPhone.

Contact

Write to lamnv260398@gmail.com. Please say which device you play on and, if it is about a page, its chapter and plate number.

How to play

Each page is a riddle sentence above a picture. Drag a word from the sentence into the picture to make it real, drag a thing from the picture up into a gap in the sentence to take it away, or drop a loose letter on a word to respell it (a G on BOAT makes a GOAT). A wrong word goes wrong; an Eraser takes the move back, and a Quill shows the next move.

Purchases

The Cottage is free. The Whole Book opens The Harbour, The Market and The Lighthouse, once, and adds five Quills. An Inkwell adds 12 Quills and 6 Erasers. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
