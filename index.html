How It Plays

  1. I

    Read the riddle sentence under the plate number.

  2. II

    Change the page in one of three ways.

    Make

    Drag RAIN from the sentence onto the sky, and it rains on the garden.

    Take

    Drag the tree from the picture up into the gap in the sentence, and the tree is gone.

    Respell

    Drop a G on BOAT. Now it is a GOAT, standing where the boat was.

  3. III

    The page comes true: So it is.

On Every Plate

Oops

A wrong word goes wrong: the cat gets soaked, the ships follow the moon onto the rocks. An Eraser takes the move back.

The Quill

Stuck? The Quill outlines the next word to move and marks where it goes.

Forty Plates

Hand-made pages in four chapters: The Cottage, The Harbour, The Market, The Lighthouse.
