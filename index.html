A Riddle Book in Four Chapters

Nounsmith

or, Words That Become Things

Drag a word in. It becomes REAL.

Nounsmith is a book of 40 word riddles for iPhone. Each page is a sentence set in wood type above a printed picture, and the words and the things trade places.

No ads · No tracking · No timers · Plays offline

Three Ways to Change a Page

Make

Drag RAIN from the sentence onto the sky, and it rains on the garden.

Take

Drag the tree from the picture up into the gap in the sentence, and the tree is gone.

Respell

Drop a G on BOAT. Now it is a GOAT, standing where the boat was.

On Every Plate

Oops

A wrong word goes wrong: the kite blows away, the ship follows the moon onto the rocks. An Eraser takes the move back.

The Quill

Stuck? The Quill points at the next word to move and where it goes.

Forty Plates

Hand-made pages in four chapters: The Cottage, The Harbour, The Market, The Lighthouse.

How It Plays

I

Read the riddle sentence under the plate number.

II

Drag a word into the picture, a thing into the sentence, or a letter onto a word.

III

The page comes true: So it is.

The Price

The Cottage (10 pages) is free. The Whole Book opens the other three chapters for $2.99, once. An Inkwell of 12 Quills and 6 Erasers is $1.99. Prices may vary by country.

Questions

Is there a timer or lives?
No. Take as long as a page needs.
Does it show ads or track me?
No ads, no analytics, no account. Progress stays on your iPhone.
Do I need to buy hints?
No. You start with Quills and Erasers and find more as you solve pages; the Inkwell is optional.
